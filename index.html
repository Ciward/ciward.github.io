About

Research with a builder’s mindset.

I am interested in how intelligent systems can plan, collaborate, use tools, and allocate model resources under real cost and performance constraints. My current work sits at the intersection of agentic AI, efficient foundation-model systems, and model-service economics.

Outside research, I enjoy building and shipping products. I founded TokenLab, and I am especially drawn to entrepreneurship where infrastructure, AI capability, and user experience meet.
